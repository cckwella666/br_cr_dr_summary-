1
Core Teaching: br / cr / dr核心教學：br / cr / dr
0:00 – 0:35 · 35 min分鐘
Main Lesson主要課程
Teaching Method — repeat for each blend, ~11 min教學方法 — 每組重複一次，約11分鐘
  1. Isolate both sounds. Say each letter sound separately first ("b...r"), then say them fast together ("br"). Both letters are still heard.拆解兩個音。先分開唸兩個字母的音（「b...r」），再快速合起來（「br」）。兩個字母的音都還聽得到。
  2. Trace & say. Show the blend card. Child traces the two letters while saying the blend aloud.描寫並發音。出示字母組合卡，孩子描寫兩個字母，同時唸出混合音。
  3. Oral blending. Say the blend + rest of word in two chunks, child blends: "br...ush → ?" (brush). 3–4 words per blend before showing print.口頭拼讀。把單字分成兩段唸，孩子拼起來：「br...ush → ?」（brush）。每組先做3–4個口頭練習，再出示文字卡。
  4. Read the word cards. Show all 5 word cards for that blend. Child reads each aloud. Correct by re-blending, not by giving the answer.讀字卡。出示該組的5張字卡，孩子逐一唸出。唸錯時用重新拼讀引導，不直接給答案。
  5. Sentence practice. Read the practice sentence together, child points to the blend word(s).句子練習。一起讀練習句，孩子指出含有該混合音的單字。
💡 Between blends, do a 30-second stand-up reset — e.g. "brush your teeth" for br, "crab walk" for cr, "drum on your knees" for dr — to keep focus up across all three. 💡 每教完一組，站起來做30秒的小動作轉換——例如 br 做「刷牙」、cr 做「螃蟹走路」、dr 做「在膝蓋上打鼓」——讓孩子三組都能保持專注。

Trick-or-Treat Bingo萬聖節賓果
0:35 – 0:45 · 10 min分鐘
Practice Game練習遊戲
Game遊戲
Trick-or-Treat Bingo — br / cr / dr萬聖節賓果 — br / cr / dr

Materials: the two Trick-or-Treat Bingo boards (Tyson's + Keira's) + counters. Each board has all 15 new words.
How to run: call a word from today's br/cr/dr set. Both children find it on their own board, read it aloud, and cover it. First to complete a row, column, or diagonal calls "Trick or Treat!" instead of "Bingo!" — winner gets a "treat" sticker. 教具：兩張萬聖節賓果卡（Tyson 的 + Keira 的）+ 計數物。每張卡都有全部15個新單字。
玩法：唸出今天 br/cr/dr 的單字。兩個孩子在自己的卡上找到、唸出來並蓋住。第一個連成一排、一列或對角線的人喊「Trick or Treat!」代替「Bingo!」——贏家得到一張「treat」貼紙。

💡 After they cover a word, ask "Which blend?" — a quick way to check they can tell br, cr and dr apart. 💡 孩子蓋住單字後，問一句「是哪個混合音？」——快速確認他們分得出 br、cr、dr。

4
Flashlight Detective — Blend Review手電筒偵探 — 混合音複習
0:55 – 1:05 · 10 min分鐘
Review Game複習遊戲

Reviews today's three new blends. Six words (2 each of br, cr, dr) are hidden underneath 6 different Halloween doodles — a pumpkin, ghost, Frankenstein, cauldron, haunted house and black cat. Kids shine a flashlight from behind the paper to reveal the word inside each doodle. 複習今天教的三組新混合音。6個單字（br、cr、dr 各2個）藏在6個不同的萬聖節塗鴉底下——南瓜、幽靈、科學怪人、魔法大鍋、鬼屋和黑貓。孩子用手電筒從紙的背面照，就能看到每個塗鴉裡藏的單字。

Prep (before class)課前準備
  1. Print both sheets on thin, plain printer paper — Sheet 1 (words) and Sheet 2 (doodles). Thick paper blocks the light.列印兩張紙，用一般薄的影印紙——第1張（單字）和第2張（塗鴉）。厚紙會擋住光線。
  2. Glue them together. Doodle sheet on top, word sheet underneath, both facing up. Line up the corner marks. Glue only around the edges so the glue doesn't blur the words.黏合兩張紙。塗鴉紙在上、單字紙在下，兩張都正面朝上，對齊四個角的記號。只在邊緣上膠，避免膠水讓單字變模糊。
Game (~5 min)遊戲（約5分鐘）
Flashlight Detective手電筒偵探

Hidden words: crab, brush, dragon, crown, bridge, drum.
Materials: the glued sheet + a flashlight or phone light.
How to run: dim the room a little. Hold the sheet up and shine the flashlight from behind it, one doodle at a time. The hidden word appears inside the doodle. The detective reads it aloud and says which blend it starts with (br, cr or dr). Take turns: one child holds the light, the other is the detective. 隱藏單字：crab、brush、dragon、crown、bridge、drum。
教具：黏好的遊戲紙 + 手電筒或手機手電筒。
玩法：把房間燈稍微調暗。把紙拿起來，手電筒從背面一次照一個塗鴉，藏在塗鴉裡的單字就會出現。偵探唸出單字，並說出開頭是哪個混合音（br、cr 或 dr）。輪流進行：一個孩子拿手電筒，另一個當偵探。

💡 Before each reveal, let them guess what's hiding in the doodle — the guessing builds the excitement. If the words show through too much in normal light, glue one blank sheet in between the two layers. 💡 每次照之前，先讓孩子猜猜塗鴉裡藏了什麼——猜測能讓氣氛更興奮。如果平常光線下就看得到單字，可以在兩層之間多黏一張白紙。
After the Hunt (~5 min)搜查之後（約5分鐘）
Detective Case File偵探紀錄單

Materials: one Detective Case File worksheet per child + pencils.
How to run: hand it out only after all 6 words are found, since the words are printed on it. Part 1: for each doodle, the child circles the blend (br / cr / dr), traces the hidden word, and colors the star. Part 2: they draw one thing they found and say the sentence "I found a ___." out loud. 教具：每人一張偵探紀錄單 + 鉛筆。
玩法：6個單字都找到之後才發下去，因為紀錄單上印有答案。第1部分：每個塗鴉旁，孩子圈出混合音（br / cr / dr）、描寫藏起來的單字，再把星星塗上顏色。第2部分：畫出一個找到的東西，並大聲說出「I found a ___.」。

💡 Running short on time? Do Part 1 in class and send Part 2 home as a small take-home task. 💡 時間不夠的話，課堂上先完成第1部分，第2部分當作回家的小任務。
